## In class — Bug bash

Students swap games and try to break them for fifteen minutes, then swap again. Every bug is written down with the steps that caused it. Each developer leaves with a list, triages it, and fixes the must-fix items before Gold.

## Lab goals

- Triage the bug bash list: must fix, should fix, won't fix.
- Fix the must-fix bugs first. Fixes and polish only; no new content.
- Go through the polish checklist: menus, volume levels, a way to quit, the credits screen.
- Check your credits file against every asset in your project.
