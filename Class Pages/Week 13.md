# Week 13 — Playtest

**GAME 1303**

You can't test your own game. You know where everything is, what every button does and what you meant. Today three people who know none of that play your game while you watch, and you find out what you actually built.

## Exercise — Ex 10 — Playtest

Three classmates play your game while you watch and take notes without helping. Then you turn what you saw into tasks on your board.

Open the version for your section:

- [Ex 10 — Playtest — Unity](https://github.com/Animation-GameArt/IntroToGameDesign-course/blob/main/Exercises/Ex10%20-%20Playtest.md)

Worth 2 points: one for **Implementation** (it runs, and the feature works), one for the **Design decision** (a deliberate, defensible choice beyond the defaults). Due at the end of the class period. Late work earns the Implementation point only.

**This week's design decision:** Choose the three changes that matter most from what you observed, and say why those three.

## Lab goals

- Add your three chosen changes to your board as tasks, at the top.
- Start on the one that affected the most players.
- Add any bugs the playtesters found to your bug list, and triage them.
- Back up before you leave.
