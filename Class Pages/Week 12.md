## In class — Alpha check

During studio time the instructor plays each game for a few minutes against the student's must-have list. Each feature is marked as there, partly there or missing. A missing feature is either finished this week or cut, and the student decides which today.

## Lab goals

- No new features from today. If it isn't in the Alpha, it's cut.
- List every placeholder: art, sound and text. Replace them in order of how often the player sees them.
- Play your own first minute as a new player. Does the game teach its controls and its goal without you explaining?
- Write three questions you want next week's playtest to answer.
