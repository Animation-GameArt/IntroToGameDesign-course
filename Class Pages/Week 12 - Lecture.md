# Week 12 — Studio: Alpha

**Also due:** Alpha, uploaded by 11:59 PM the night before class: every must-have feature exists and works.

## Topics, expanded

**Alpha and the feature freeze.** Alpha is a line. Before it, the question is "what else can I add?" After it, the question is "what's left to finish?" A feature freeze means no new features after today. Students resist this, because the next idea always seems small. Hold the line: the weeks after Alpha are for content, fixes and polish, and there are only three of them.

**From placeholder to final.** Have each student list every placeholder in their game. Then order the list by how often the player sees or hears each thing. The player character and the main sound come first. A background detail seen once comes last, and may ship as it is. Every asset that isn't the student's own goes into their credits file the moment it's added.

**Teaching the player in the first minute.** Return to teach and test from Week 5, applied to the student's own opening. Does the first screen let the player try the main action safely? Is the goal visible? A game that needs its developer standing beside it to explain the controls will fail next week's playtest, where nobody is allowed to explain anything.

## Connecting it forward

The three playtest questions written in lab are the plan for Week 13. Alpha is also the last point at which cutting a feature is cheap. After it, anything unfinished costs polish time.
