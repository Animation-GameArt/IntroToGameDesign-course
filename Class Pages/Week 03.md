# Week 3 — Pitch Day & Unity Fundamentals

**GAME 1303**

Today you pitch your game to the room. Then we open up Unity's building blocks, and you connect objects that have never heard of each other.

## Exercise — Ex 02 — Events

A ball rolls down a ramp and collects coins. A variable asset holds the score, a text shows it, and a key raises an event that opens a door.

Open the version for your section:

- [Ex 02 — Events — Unity](https://github.com/Animation-GameArt/IntroToGameDesign-course/blob/main/Exercises/Ex02%20-%20Events.md)

Worth 2 points: one for **Implementation** (it runs, and the feature works), one for the **Design decision** (a deliberate, defensible choice beyond the defaults). Due at the end of the class period. Late work earns the Implementation point only.

**This week's design decision:** Add one reaction of your own to an event or a variable, and be ready to say why the object that causes it doesn't need to know about the object that reacts.

## Lab goals

- Write down the feedback from your pitch while it's fresh.
- Decide what changes. A pitch that was approved with conditions needs those conditions met in the one-sheet.
- Start the one-sheet template. It's due next week.
