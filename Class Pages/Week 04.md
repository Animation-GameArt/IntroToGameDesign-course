# Week 4 — Input & Game Feel

**GAME 1303**

Two games can have the same jump and feel nothing alike. Today you put a character under your control, then tune the numbers that decide whether it feels heavy, sharp, floaty or fair.

## Exercise — Ex 03 — Player

Replace the ball with a character, build a small level with platforms, tune the movement, then swap in a top-down controller.

Open the version for your section:

- [Ex 03 — Player — Unity](https://github.com/Animation-GameArt/IntroToGameDesign-course/blob/main/Exercises/Ex03%20-%20Player.md)

Worth 2 points: one for **Implementation** (it runs, and the feature works), one for the **Design decision** (a deliberate, defensible choice beyond the defaults). Due at the end of the class period. Late work earns the Implementation point only.

**This week's design decision:** Tune at least three movement settings away from their defaults to give your character a specific feel, and name the feel you were going for.
