# Week 5 — Levels & Cameras

**GAME 1303**

A level is a lesson plan the player doesn't know they're following. Today you build one that teaches a skill before it tests it, and give the player a camera that shows them what they need to see.

## Exercise — Ex 04 — Level

Build a short level wider than the screen, with a following camera, camera bounds, hazards and checkpoints.

Open the version for your section:

- [Ex 04 — Level — Unity](https://github.com/Animation-GameArt/IntroToGameDesign-course/blob/main/Exercises/Ex04%20-%20Level.md)

Worth 2 points: one for **Implementation** (it runs, and the feature works), one for the **Design decision** (a deliberate, defensible choice beyond the defaults). Due at the end of the class period. Late work earns the Implementation point only.

**This week's design decision:** Build one **teach beat** and one **test beat**: a safe place where the player learns a skill, then a place that demands it.
