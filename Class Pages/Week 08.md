# Week 8 — Juice, Audio & Story

**GAME 1303**

Your game starts today. The sandbox is finished, your ten-pager is approved, and from here on every class hour goes into your own project. Today's topic is what makes a working game feel good: sound, feedback and a little story.

## Exercise — Ex 07 — Juice & Audio

In your own game: add sound and visual feedback to your three most common actions, add background music, and add one camera shake.

Open the version for your section:

- [Ex 07 — Juice & Audio — Unity](https://github.com/Animation-GameArt/IntroToGameDesign-course/blob/main/Exercises/Ex07%20-%20Juice%20%26%20Audio.md)

Worth 2 points: one for **Implementation** (it runs, and the feature works), one for the **Design decision** (a deliberate, defensible choice beyond the defaults). Due at the end of the class period. Late work earns the Implementation point only.

**This week's design decision:** Choose the single moment in your game that deserves the biggest reaction, and build that reaction.
