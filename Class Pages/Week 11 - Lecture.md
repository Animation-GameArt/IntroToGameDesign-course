# Week 11 — Studio: Sprint review

**Also due:** the sprint review build, uploaded by 11:59 PM the night before class, and a two-minute demo in class.

## Topics, expanded

**Stand-ups.** Every studio session starts the same way. Each student answers three questions in under a minute: what did I finish, what am I doing today, and what is blocking me. The third question is the useful one. A blocked student gets help in the first ten minutes and not in the last ten. Keep it standing and keep it short.

**What "feels right" means.** The core loop is the thing the player does most, so it's worth more attention than anything else in the game. "Feels right" can be tested: the main action responds at once, the player can tell what happened, and doing it a twentieth time is still satisfying. If a student can't play their own loop for two minutes without getting bored, more content won't help.

**A tuning pass.** Tuning is changing numbers, not adding features. Pick the main action. Change one value at a time while the game runs, as in Exercise 03. Write down the values that work, because changes made during Play are lost. Thirty minutes of tuning often improves a game more than a week of new features.

## Connecting it forward

The sprint review is the first of four in-class checks: sprint review, Alpha, the playtest and Beta. Whatever a student named as not feeling right today should be the first thing visibly fixed in the Alpha build.
