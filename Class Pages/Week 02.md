# Week 2 — Pitch & Plan

**GAME 1303**

An idea becomes a project when someone else understands it and you know what you'll cut. Today you learn to pitch, to scope and to keep your work safe, and you open the course project for the first time.

## Exercise — Ex 01 — Bootstrap

Download the course project, add it in Unity Hub, set up your backup, and make two scenes that switch with a fade.

Open the version for your section:

- [Ex 01 — Bootstrap — Unity](https://github.com/Animation-GameArt/IntroToGameDesign-course/blob/main/Exercises/Ex01%20-%20Bootstrap.md)

Worth 2 points: one for **Implementation** (it runs, and the feature works), one for the **Design decision** (a deliberate, defensible choice beyond the defaults). Due at the end of the class period. Late work earns the Implementation point only.

**This week's design decision:** Decide how your two scenes change: on a button, on a timer, or both. Be ready to say what kind of game moment each one suits.

## Lab goals

- Pick the one idea you'll pitch. Read the feedback on your Discussion post first.
- Write your pitch post: a logline, the core loop, what makes it different, and any artwork you have.
- Check the idea against the supported genres. If it doesn't fit one, talk to your instructor today.
