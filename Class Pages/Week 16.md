# Week 16 — Post-Mortem

**GAME 1303**

Sixteen weeks ago you had three one-line ideas. Today you have a released game. This last class is for looking at how that happened: what worked, what didn't, and what you'd do differently next time.

## In class — Presentations

Each student has five minutes: one minute showing the game, then what went right, what went wrong, what they'd change, and how the finished game compares with the scope in their ten-pager. Two minutes of questions follow.

## Lab goals

- Listen for problems you also had. Most of them are shared.
- Write down one practice from someone else's project that you'll use in your next one.
- Make sure your itch.io page and your project backup are both in a place you'll still have next semester.
