# Week 1 — Games & Genres

**GAME 1303**

Every game is a small set of actions repeated until they get interesting. Today you learn to name that loop in the games you already play, see how genres group those loops, and get Unity running.

**This week's design decision:** Arrange a few sprites in your practice scene so that it suggests one of your game ideas, and say what the idea is.

## Lab goals

- Write a one-line core loop for three games you know well. It's practice for your own ideas.
- Draft your three game ideas. One line each: who the player is, what they do, and what makes it fun.
- Choose ideas that fit a genre on the genre map. You'll build one of them.
