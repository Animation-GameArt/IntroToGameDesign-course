# Genre Selection

Tick every genre that students may pitch in the **first semester**. Each ticked genre gets a place on the student Genre Map and a kit. Anything left unticked isn't offered yet.

**How to read each line**

- ★ marks the genres recommended for the first semester.
- **Effort** is the work to build and test the kit: *small* (the sandbox exercises already cover most of it), *medium* (one new kit), *large* (several new systems, or a genre that is hard to finish in eight weeks).
- Genres that share a kit are noted, so ticking both costs little more than ticking one.

## Notes

- The ★ set is ten kits: nine in 2D and the visual novel. Three of them (platformer, top-down adventure, turn-based battle) are small because the sandbox already contains most of their scripts.
- 3D, 2.5D, VR and AR all wait on a 3D template project. Ticking any genre in those groups puts the 3D template ahead of some of the 2D kits.
- A genre left off the map can still be allowed case by case, with instructor approval and no kit.
