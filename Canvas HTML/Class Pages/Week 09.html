Exercise — Ex 08 — Save & Build

In your own game: save and load one thing that matters, add a volume setting, then make a build and upload it to a private itch.io page.

Open the version for your section:

Worth 2 points: one for Implementation (it runs, and the feature works), one for the Design decision (a deliberate, defensible choice beyond the defaults). Due at the end of the class period. Late work earns the Implementation point only.

This week's design decision: Decide what your game saves and when it saves it, and be ready to say what the player would lose without it.

Lab goals

  • Fix whatever the first build revealed. Something always behaves differently outside the Editor.
  • Ask another student to play your build for two minutes. Watch, and don't explain anything.
  • Keep working toward First Playable: one complete pass through your core loop.
  • Back up before you leave.
