Topics, expanded

Beta: content complete. At Alpha the features were finished. At Beta the content is: every level is built, every placeholder is replaced or deliberately kept, and the game can be played from the first screen to the last. The rule from here to Gold is fixes and polish only. A new level added now will be the least tested part of the game.

A bug bash. Other people break a game in ways its developer never would, because they don't know which paths are safe. Fifteen minutes per game, and every bug gets written down with its steps. A report that says "it broke" is worthless; "jump while paused, then resume" is a gift. Developers shouldn't defend or explain during the bash. They should write.

The polish checklist. Small things decide whether a game feels finished. The title screen shows the game's name. Every menu can be left. Music and sound effects are at sensible levels against each other. The game can be quit. Text is readable. The window fits the screen. None of these takes long, and a missing one is the first thing a player notices.

Credits and licenses. Every asset the student didn't make needs an entry: its name, its author, where it came from and its license. Check that each license allows the use. CC0 and CC BY are fine, with credit given for CC BY. An asset with no clear license comes out of the game. This is also the moment to make sure the student's own name is on the credits screen.
