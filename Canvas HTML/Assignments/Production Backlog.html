GAME 1303 · Due by 11:59 PM the night before the Week 8 class · Points: TBD

Turn your ten-pager into a plan you can work from: every feature broken into tasks on a board, each with a week.

What to submit

A link to your task board, or screenshots of it, in Canvas.

Requirements

Each line is a graded criterion.

  • The board has three columns: To Do, Doing and Done.
  • Every must-have feature from page 10 of your ten-pager appears as one or more tasks.
  • Each task is small enough to finish in one sitting, about three hours or less.
  • Each task names a week, from 8 to 15.
  • Art and sound are on the board too: one task per asset or group of assets.
  • Nice-to-have features are listed separately, below the must-haves.
  • The milestones are marked: First Playable (Week 10), Alpha (Week 12), Beta (Week 14), Gold (Week 15).

Tips

  • Start from your ten-pager's must-have list and work down it one line at a time.
  • If a task starts with "finish" or "make the," it's probably too big. Split it.
  • The first tasks should get your player moving in your own scene.
  • A board in a notes app, a spreadsheet or on paper is fine. What matters is that you update it.

Grading

Points are to be assigned. Each requirement above is checked as met or not met.
