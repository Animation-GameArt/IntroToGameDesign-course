GAME 1303 · Week 1 · Canvas Discussion · Due by 11:59 PM on the day of the first class · Points: TBD

Three one-line ideas for the game you might make this semester. One of them becomes your pitch in Week 3.

Scope

This is brainstorming. You aren't committing to anything yet, and nothing needs to be worked out in detail.

Scope
In scope Out of scope
Three short, different ideas Story, characters or art
A genre from the Genre Map for each Mechanics lists and level plans
Replying to classmates Choosing your final idea

What to submit

A post in the Canvas Discussion with three ideas. For each idea, write:

  1. One line: who the player is, what they do, and what makes it fun. For example: "A raccoon steals snacks from a campsite without waking the campers."
  2. Genre: one genre from the Genre Map.

Then reply to two classmates' posts. Say which of their ideas you'd most want to play, and why.

Requirements

Each line is a graded criterion.

  • Three ideas, each one line.
  • Each idea names a genre from the Genre Map.
  • The three ideas are different from each other, not three versions of one game.
  • Two replies to classmates, each saying which idea and why.

Tips

  • Write the line around what the player does. See Core Loops.
  • If you love a genre that the Genre Map rates "ambitious," make one of your ideas a small version of it, and another a "good first game."
  • If your favorite idea isn't on the map, post it anyway and ask in class. Ideas off the map are discussed before pitch week.
