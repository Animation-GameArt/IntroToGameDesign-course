GAME 1303 · Week 2 · In person, by the end of class · Points: TBD

A quick check that the course project runs on your computer or SSD, and that your work is backed up.

Scope

Scope
In scope Out of scope
The course project opens with no errors Any work on your own game
A working backup method Choosing your game idea

What to show your instructor

  1. The course project open in Unity 6000.3.10f1, with no red errors in the Console.
  2. Where the project lives: your laptop or your SSD.
  3. Your backup method, working:
    • Unity Version Control: the project connected, with one check-in made today; or
    • SSD: the project on the SSD, and your plan for a weekly copy to a second device at home.

Requirements

  • The course project opens in Unity 6000.3.10f1.
  • The Console shows no red errors.
  • The project is on your laptop or SSD, not in a cloud-synced folder.
  • Your backup method is set up and you can explain it.
