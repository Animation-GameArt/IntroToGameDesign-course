All content is in. From here to release, you only fix and polish.

What to submit

The link to your Beta build, in Canvas, with a note listing the three changes you made after the playtest.

Requirements

Each line is a graded criterion.

  • Every level is finished and in the build.
  • Final art and sound are in. Any placeholder that remains is a deliberate choice.
  • The three changes you chose after the playtest are made.
  • The credits screen is complete, with a name, author, source and license for every asset you didn't make.
  • The game has volume control and a way to quit.
  • There is no known crash, and no known way to get permanently stuck.
  • Your bug list is triaged: must fix, should fix, won't fix.

Tips

  • Check each asset's license. If you can't find one, replace the asset.
  • Play from the first screen to the last without stopping, twice.

Grading

Points are to be assigned. Each requirement above is checked as met or not met.
