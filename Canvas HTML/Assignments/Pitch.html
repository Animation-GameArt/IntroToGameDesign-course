GAME 1303 · Week 3 · Canvas Discussion, due by 11:59 PM the night before class · Presented live in class · Points: TBD

A written pitch for the one game you want to make, posted before class and presented live from your post. Pitch Day ends with a decision: approved, approved with conditions, or revise.

Scope

The pitch says what the game is and proves it fits the course. It doesn't design the game in detail; that's the one-sheet and the ten-pager.

Scope
In scope Out of scope
One game, in one genre from the Genre Map Several game modes
The core loop and a short mechanics list Full level designs
A realistic scope for eight weeks of production Detailed story or dialogue
Any artwork you already have Finished art

What to submit

A post in the Canvas Discussion with these parts, in this order and with these headings:

  1. Title
  2. Logline: one sentence with the player, the action and the hook.
  3. Genre: one genre from the Genre Map.
  4. Core loop: the actions the player repeats, written as a short chain, such as "explore → find key → open door → fight."
  5. Mechanics: a bulleted list of everything the player can do or interact with.
  6. Must-haves: the three to five things the game can't work without.
  7. Win and lose: how the player wins, and how they fail.
  8. Comparable games: one or two, and how yours is different.
  9. Scope: how many levels, rooms, enemies, battles or scenes.
  10. Artwork: sketches, mockups or labeled reference images, if you have any. Credit anything you didn't make.

Presenting

Present from your post. You have about two minutes. There are no slides.

Requirements

Each line is a graded criterion.

  • The post is in the Discussion by 11:59 PM the night before class.
  • All nine written parts are present, under their headings.
  • The genre is on the Genre Map.
  • The core loop is one short chain of actions.
  • The mechanics list is specific enough to compare with the genre's kit.
  • The scope is stated in numbers.
  • The pitch is presented in about two minutes.

After Pitch Day

  • Approved: go on to the one-sheet.
  • Approved with conditions: the named changes must appear in your one-sheet.
  • Revise: rework and repost by the date your instructor gives.

Your mechanics list is compared with your genre's kit. Anything the kit doesn't cover is either agreed as one of your custom scripts or cut.

Tips

  • Read your genre's section of the Genre Map first, especially A good scope and Leave out.
  • Say your logline aloud to someone. If they can't repeat it back, simplify it.
  • "Three levels, two enemy types, one boss" beats "a big world full of secrets."
