GAME 1303 · Week 7 · Canvas, due by 11:59 PM the night before class · Points: 10

Your game's design document: ten pages that describe the whole game, from its outline to its technical plan. In Week 8 you build from it, starting with a backlog made from its must-have list. The format follows Scott Rogers' ten-pager in Level Up!

Scope

The ten-pager describes the game you'll actually build in eight weeks, not the biggest version you can imagine.

Scope
Page Covers
1 Title page
2 Game outline: story, flow, win and lose
3 Character and controls
4 Gameplay: loop, progression, structure, difficulty
5 Game world: locations and how they connect
6 Game experience: tone, camera, screens, UI, sound
7 Mechanics
8 Enemies and obstacles
9 Cutscenes and narrative
10 Technical plan and scope (changed from Rogers' bonus page to fit the course)

Page 10 connects your design to the course: which kit, which other packages, which custom scripts, art and audio sources, must-have and nice-to-have lists, a milestone plan, and your biggest risk.

What to submit

The final ten-pager as a PDF or Word file in Canvas.

Requirements

Each line is a graded criterion.

  • All ten pages are complete, with no guidance text left.
  • Each page is roughly one page long.
  • The design matches the approved pitch and one-sheet, or changes are explained.
  • The notes from your peer review have been addressed.
  • Page 6 includes your screen layouts.
  • Page 10 names your kit, lists any custom scripts agreed at Pitch Day, and separates must-haves from nice-to-haves.
  • Page 10 has the decisions table from your kit's Build Your Game guide, with every row filled in.
  • Page 10 has a milestone plan for weeks 10 to 15.
  • Page 10 names your biggest risk and a backup plan.
  • Every asset you plan to use that you didn't make is listed with its license.

Tips

  • Use pictures, sketches and tables wherever they explain faster than words.
  • Read your must-have list as if you had half the time. Anything that survives is a real must-have.
  • Your genre's kit guide lists what the kit does and doesn't do. Check your mechanics against it.
  • Copy the decisions table from your kit's Build Your Game guide (in Kit Guides). You'll build from it, step by step, starting in Week 8.

Reference

  • Scott Rogers, Level Up! The Guide to Great Video Game Design, 2nd ed. (Wiley, 2014).
