Every feature you're going to ship exists and works. Art, sound and balance can still be rough.

What to submit

The link to your Alpha build, in Canvas, with your must-have list marked up: there, partly there, or cut.

Requirements

Each line is a graded criterion.

  • Every must-have feature from your ten-pager is in the build, or is marked as cut with a reason.
  • Every screen exists: main menu, pause, win or lose, and credits.
  • Every level or stage is at least blocked in and can be played through.
  • The game can be played from the first screen to the last.
  • Saving works, if your game saves.
  • Your list of placeholders is written down.
  • No new features will be added after this build.

Tips

  • Rough is fine. Missing is not.
  • If a feature isn't going to make it, cut it now and say so. Cutting on purpose is a skill.

Grading

Points are to be assigned. Each requirement above is checked as met or not met.
