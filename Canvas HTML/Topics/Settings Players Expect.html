Week 9 · [Lecture] Week 9

In one sentence
A few settings are expected in every game, and players notice their absence more than they notice most features.

What this topic is for

Students add at least a volume setting in Ex 08, and plan the others for the polish checklist in Week 14.

Key ideas

Key ideas
Setting Why
Music volume and effects volume, separately Players often want one without the other
A way to quit From the menu, and from pause
Full screen or windowed (desktop builds) Players switch to answer a message
Camera shake off (if the game shakes) Comfort
  • Settings are saved so players don't set them every time. The course's Settings screen and UI Settings component provide both volumes and full screen.
  • Settings live in a menu reached from the title and from pause.
  • Controls should be listed somewhere, in the game or on the store page.

In class

  1. Open the settings of three well-known games and list what they share.
  2. Agree the minimum for this course: two volume sliders and quit.
