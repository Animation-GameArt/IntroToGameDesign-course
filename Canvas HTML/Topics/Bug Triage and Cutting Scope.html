Week 10 · [Lecture] Week 10

In one sentence
Not every bug gets fixed and not every feature gets built, and deciding which ones on purpose is what finishes games.

What this topic is for

Ex 09's design decision is a triaged bug list with one defended "won't fix." Students also cut their nice-to-have features now, deliberately, rather than losing them by accident in Week 14.

Key ideas

  • Triage sorts every bug by how bad it is and how often it happens:
Key ideas
Category Means
Must fix Crashes, getting stuck, losing progress, anything that stops play
Should fix Noticeable, but the game can still be played and finished
Won't fix Rare or minor, and the time is better spent elsewhere
  • "Won't fix" is a professional decision, not a failure. Shipped games all have known bugs.
  • Cut features the same way. Look at the nice-to-have list and decide now. The earlier a cut, the cheaper it is.
  • Milestones keep the decisions honest. First Playable, Alpha, Beta, Gold. See Task Boards and Milestones.

In class

  1. Triage a sample bug list together.
  2. Each student cuts at least one nice-to-have and removes it from their board.
