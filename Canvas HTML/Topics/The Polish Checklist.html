Week 14 · [Lecture] Week 14

In one sentence
Small things decide whether a game feels finished, and none of them takes long.

What this topic is for

A list students work through between Beta and Gold. A missing item is the first thing a player notices.

The checklist

  • The title screen shows the game's name.
  • Every menu can be left, with a mouse and with a gamepad.
  • The game can be quit from the title screen and from pause.
  • Music and effects have separate volume controls, and they're saved.
  • Music and effects are at sensible levels against each other.
  • All text is readable at the build's window size.
  • The player always knows how to win and how they're doing.
  • Winning and losing each have a screen, and both lead somewhere.
  • The credits screen is complete, including the student's own name.
  • No placeholder text such as "Game Title" or "Your Name" remains.
  • The build's name, icon and version are set.
  • The game has been played start to finish twice, in a build, without stopping.
