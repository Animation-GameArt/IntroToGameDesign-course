Week 14 · [Lecture] Week 14

In one sentence
Every asset you didn't make needs a credit and a license that allows your use, and an asset with no clear license comes out of the game.

What this topic is for

Students publish their games publicly in Week 15. Before that, every asset is checked and credited. This is professional practice, and it protects students' portfolios.

Key ideas

  • A credit names the asset, its author, where it came from, and its license.
  • Common licenses:
Key ideas
License May you use it? Credit required?
CC0 (public domain) Yes, for anything No, but credit it anyway
CC BY Yes Yes, as the license describes
CC BY-NC Only if the game is never sold Yes
"Free for personal use," or no license stated Not for a public release —
  • The course's own assets are credited already in Assets/_Course/Credits/Course Credits, including the Kenney art packs (CC0).
  • The student's credits go in Assets/_Game/Credits, which the Credits screen shows above the course's.
  • Credit as you go. Reconstructing sources at the end is slow and error-prone.
  • When unsure, replace it. There's always a CC0 alternative.
