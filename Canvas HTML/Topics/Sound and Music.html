Week 8 · [Lecture] Week 8

In one sentence
Sound does more for how a game feels per hour of work than anything else, and it needs separate controls for music and effects.

What this topic is for

Students add sound effects and music to their own game in Ex 07, from free sources with clear licenses, and set it up so the player can control the volume in Week 9.

Key ideas

  • Sound effects answer actions; music sets mood. They're mixed and controlled separately.
  • Variation. A sound that plays often should vary slightly in pitch or volume each time, or it becomes irritating.
  • Levels. Effects should be heard over the music, not drowned by it. Leave the music lower than feels natural while working.
  • The Audio Mixer groups sounds so music and effects have their own volume, which is what the settings menu controls.
  • Free sources with clear licenses. CC0 packs, such as Kenney's audio packs, need no permission. Every sound the student didn't make goes in their credits file.
  • Silence is a choice. A quiet moment makes the next sound land harder.

Course scripts
A course audio component with music crossfades, sound variation and mixer groups is planned for the Week 8 scripts.

In class

  1. Play a clip with no sound, then with sound.
  2. Play one effect ten times without variation, then with.
