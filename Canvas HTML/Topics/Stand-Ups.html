Week 11 · [Lecture] Week 11 · Instructor notes

In one sentence
Each studio session opens with every student answering three questions in under a minute, so problems surface at the start of class and not at the end.

What this is for

Stand-ups replace the lecture from Week 11 to Week 15. They keep everyone visible, and they catch blocked students early.

How it runs

  • Three questions, in under a minute each:
    1. What did I finish since last class?
    2. What am I doing today?
    3. What's blocking me?
  • Standing, and short. Discussion of a problem happens after the stand-up, with the people who need to be there.
  • The third question is the useful one. Note every blocker and visit those students first.
  • The board is the evidence. "Finished" means moved to Done on the board.

Watch for

Watch for
Signal Usually means
The same task in "doing" two classes running It's too big, or the student is stuck and not saying so
"Nothing's blocking me" every week, and little in Done Scope trouble. Talk privately
Many students blocked on the same thing A five-minute topic for the whole class
