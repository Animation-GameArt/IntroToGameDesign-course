Week 2 · [Lecture] Week 2

In one sentence
A task board shows what's left to do and a milestone says when it must be done, and together they keep a solo project moving.

What this topic is for

Students won't build a full backlog until Week 8, but they should know now what one is and why the course's milestones fall where they do. Introducing it early means the ten-pager is written with tasks and weeks in mind.

Key ideas

  • A three-column board: To Do, Doing, Done. Any tool works: a notes app, a spreadsheet, sticky notes on a wall. What matters is that it's updated.
  • Small tasks. A task should take one sitting, about three hours or less. "Make the level" isn't a task; "paint the first screen of level 1" is.
  • One thing in Doing. Working on several tasks at once feels productive and finishes nothing.
  • Milestones in this course:
Key ideas
Milestone Week Means
First Playable 10 The core loop works once, start to finish
Sprint review 11 The core loop is complete and feels right
Alpha 12 Every feature exists, even if rough
Beta 14 All content is in; only fixes from here
Gold 15 Released on a public page
  • Milestones are checks, not deadlines to cram for. Each one is due the night before class, so class time is for feedback and building.

In class

  1. Show a small board with a handful of tasks for a sample game.
  2. Rewrite two "too big" tasks into small ones together.
