# Gold

**GAME 1303 · Due by 11:59 PM the night before the Week 15 class · Points: 10**

Your finished game, released on a public itch.io page.

## What to submit

The link to your public page, in Canvas.

## Requirements

Each line is a graded criterion.

- [ ] The page is public, and the game can be downloaded or played from it.
- [ ] The page has the game's title and one sentence saying what the player does.
- [ ] The page has at least three screenshots of the game being played.
- [ ] The page lists the controls.
- [ ] The page has credits, or says where in the game to find them.
- [ ] The build is version 1.0, and you have run it on a second computer.
- [ ] Every must-fix bug from the bug bash is fixed.

## Tips

- Use the logline from your one-sheet as the page's first sentence.
- Ask someone who hasn't seen the game to find it, start it and play for a minute, without help.

## Grading

Points are to be assigned. Each requirement above is checked as met or not met.
