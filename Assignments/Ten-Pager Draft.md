# Ten-Pager Draft

**GAME 1303 · Week 6 · Canvas, due by 11:59 PM the night before class · Peer review in lab · Points: TBD**

A first draft of your ten-pager. It doesn't need to be finished, but every page needs something on it, so a classmate can review the whole design in lab.

## Scope

| In scope | Out of scope |
|---|---|
| All ten pages started | Polished writing |
| Page 1 complete, from your one-sheet | Final images |
| Your screen layouts on page 6 | |
| A first version of page 10's must-have list | |

## Templates

| Format | File |
|---|---|
| Word | [Ten-Pager Template.docx](https://github.com/Animation-GameArt/IntroToGameDesign-course/blob/main/Templates/Ten-Pager%20Template.docx) |
| Markdown (Obsidian) | [Ten-Pager Template.md](https://github.com/Animation-GameArt/IntroToGameDesign-course/blob/main/Templates/Ten-Pager%20Template.md) |

## What to submit

The draft as a PDF or Word file in Canvas. Bring it to class too, on screen or printed.

## Requirements

Each line is a graded criterion.

- [ ] All ten pages have content. Notes and bullet points are fine.
- [ ] Page 1 is complete.
- [ ] Page 6 includes your screen layouts.
- [ ] Page 10 has a must-have list and names the course kit you'll use.
- [ ] In lab, you review a classmate's draft and give two specific notes: one thing that's clear, and one thing you couldn't picture.
