# Post-Mortem

**GAME 1303 · Due by 11:59 PM the night before the Week 16 class · Points: TBD**

A five-minute presentation that looks back honestly at your project: what worked, what didn't, and what you'd change.

## What to submit

Your slides, in Canvas. You present them in class.

## Requirements

Each line is a graded criterion.

- [ ] You show the game for about one minute.
- [ ] Three things that went right, and why.
- [ ] Three things that went wrong, and why.
- [ ] What you would do differently, said specifically enough to act on.
- [ ] Your ten-pager's scope page compared with the finished game: what was cut, what was added, what changed.
- [ ] One thing you learned that you'll use on your next project.
- [ ] The presentation fits in five minutes.

## Tips

- Be specific. "I ran out of time" is a symptom; say what the time went to.
- This is graded on honesty and insight, not on how well the project went.

## Grading

Points are to be assigned. Each requirement above is checked as met or not met.
