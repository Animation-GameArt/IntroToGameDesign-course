# Screen Layout

**GAME 1303 · Week 5 · Canvas, due by 11:59 PM the night before class · Points: TBD**

Sketches of your game's main screens, showing what the player sees and where. In Week 7 you'll build screens like these in UI Toolkit, and the HUD sketch goes on page 6 of your ten-pager.

## Scope

| In scope | Out of scope |
|---|---|
| Four screens: title, HUD during play, pause, win or lose | Final art or fonts |
| What each screen shows, and where | Animation |
| Labels explaining each element | Every menu your game might ever need |
| Paper or digital | Building anything in Unity |

## What to submit

One image or PDF per screen, or one page with all four, in Canvas. Photos of paper sketches are fine if they're readable.

For each screen:

1. A sketch of the layout at the game's screen shape (wide, 16:9).
2. A label on every element: what it shows, and why the player needs it.
3. For the HUD, a note on which elements are **screen UI** and which, if any, are **in the game world**, such as a health bar above a character.

## Requirements

Each line is a graded criterion.

- [ ] All four screens: title, HUD, pause, and win or lose.
- [ ] Every element is labeled.
- [ ] The HUD shows only what the player needs during play, and each element has a reason.
- [ ] Every screen has a way to leave it (except the HUD).
- [ ] The layouts match the game in your one-sheet.

## Tips

- Start from what the player must know at every moment, such as health, score or time. Put the most important thing where the eye goes first.
- Leave the middle of the HUD clear. That's where the game is.
- Look at two or three games in your genre and note where they put the same information.
