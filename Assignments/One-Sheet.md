# One-Sheet

**GAME 1303 · Week 4 · Canvas, due by 11:59 PM the night before class · Points: 5**

Your approved pitch, rewritten as a single page that someone could read in two minutes and understand your game. The one-sheet is the industry's standard approval document, following Scott Rogers' format in *Level Up!*

## Scope

The one-sheet sells the game. It's short on purpose.

| In scope | Out of scope |
|---|---|
| Exactly one page | Level-by-level design (that's the ten-pager) |
| The game's title, genre, audience and rating | Technical details |
| Logline, story summary, core loop | A full feature list |
| Three unique selling points | |
| Comparable games and one key image | |
| Any conditions from Pitch Day, met | |

## Templates

Use either template. They have the same sections.

| Format | File |
|---|---|
| Word | [One-Sheet Template.docx](https://github.com/Animation-GameArt/IntroToGameDesign-course/blob/main/Templates/One-Sheet%20Template.docx) |
| Markdown (Obsidian) | [One-Sheet Template.md](https://github.com/Animation-GameArt/IntroToGameDesign-course/blob/main/Templates/One-Sheet%20Template.md) |

Delete the grey guidance text before you submit.

## What to submit

The one-sheet as a PDF or Word file in Canvas.

## Requirements

Each line is a graded criterion.

- [ ] It fits on one page.
- [ ] Every section of the template is filled in, with no guidance text left.
- [ ] The genre matches your approved pitch.
- [ ] The core loop is stated in one line.
- [ ] Three unique selling points that a player would notice.
- [ ] One or two comparable games, with how yours is different.
- [ ] A key image, credited if you didn't make it.
- [ ] Any conditions from Pitch Day are met.

## Tips

- Write for a busy reader who decides in two minutes.
- Unique selling points are things a player would notice, not technical features.
- Your logline can improve from the pitch. Keep the best version; it goes on your store page in Week 15.

## Reference

- Scott Rogers, [Level Up! The Guide to Great Video Game Design](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/-/9781118877197/), 2nd ed. (Wiley, 2014), introduces the one-sheet and the ten-pager. The course's templates follow its format, with page 10 of the ten-pager changed to fit the course.
