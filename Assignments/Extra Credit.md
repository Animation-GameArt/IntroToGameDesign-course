# Extra Credit

**GAME 1303 · Any week · Canvas, by the last class · Up to 5 extra points**

Earn extra points by going to program and industry events. They're where you meet working developers, other students and future teammates, and see what people are making.

## Events that count

| Event | When and where |
|---|---|
| Guest Speaker Series | As announced |
| Portfolio Review | As announced |
| Media Arts Student Showcase | In the spring |
| Industry Insights | Monthly, on Saturdays. Free |
| Dallas Society of Play meetings | In person or on Discord |
| Game Developers of Dallas meetings | In person or on Discord |
| Global Game Jam | In January, at Collin College |
| Other game jams at the college | As announced |
| Student Animators & Game Artists | Meetings, tours and special events |

Other networking events may count too. Ask your instructor before you go.

## How to claim it

For each event, post in the Extra Credit assignment in Canvas:

1. The event's name and date.
2. A photo of you there, or a screenshot if it was online.
3. Two or three sentences: one thing you learned, or one person you met and what they work on.

How many points each event earns is announced in Canvas. The total is capped at 5 extra points.

## Requirements

- [ ] Each event is named and dated.
- [ ] Each event has a photo or screenshot.
- [ ] Each event has a short note on what you learned or who you met.
- [ ] Everything is posted by 11:59 PM the night before the last class.
