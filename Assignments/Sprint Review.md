# Sprint Review

**GAME 1303 · Due by 11:59 PM the night before the Week 11 class · Points: TBD**

Your core loop, complete and feeling right, shown to the class in a two-minute demo.

## What to submit

The link to your updated build, in Canvas. You also demo the game in class.

## Requirements

Each line is a graded criterion.

- [ ] The core loop is complete: the player can do everything the loop needs, start to finish.
- [ ] You have tuned the main action. Its settings are no longer the defaults, and you can say why.
- [ ] The three most common actions have feedback: a sound, an effect, or both.
- [ ] The HUD shows what the player needs to know.
- [ ] You have a known-issues list of your five most important bugs.
- [ ] Your board is up to date.
- [ ] In your demo, you play the loop once and name one thing that feels right and one that doesn't.

## Tips

- Rehearse the demo. Two minutes is about one play-through and two sentences.
- Choose the "doesn't feel right" item honestly. It's the most useful thing you'll say.

## Grading

Points are to be assigned. Each requirement above is checked as met or not met.
