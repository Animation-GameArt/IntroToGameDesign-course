# Replacing Placeholders

**Week 12 · [[Lecture] Week 12](https://github.com/Animation-GameArt/IntroToGameDesign-course/blob/main/Class%20Pages/Week%2012%20-%20Lecture.md)**

> [!abstract] In one sentence
> List every placeholder, then replace them in order of how often the player sees or hears each one.

## What this topic is for

After Alpha, most of the remaining work is content. Students need an order to do it in, so the most visible parts of the game are finished first.

## Key ideas

- **Make the list.** Every placeholder sprite, sound, piece of text and level.
- **Order by exposure.** The player character and its main sound are seen and heard constantly; replace them first. A background detail seen once comes last, and may ship as it is.
- **Consistency beats quality.** A game whose art all comes from one style looks more finished than one with a few beautiful pieces among placeholders. Asset packs from one creator help.
- **Credit as you go.** Add every asset to the credits file the moment it goes into the project, with its license.
- **Check import settings.** Pixel art needs point filtering and no compression, and the right pixels-per-unit, or it looks blurry or the wrong size.

## In class

1. Each student lists their placeholders and numbers them by exposure.
