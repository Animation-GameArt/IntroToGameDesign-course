# Alpha and the Feature Freeze

**Week 12 · [[Lecture] Week 12](https://github.com/Animation-GameArt/IntroToGameDesign-course/blob/main/Class%20Pages/Week%2012%20-%20Lecture.md)**

> [!abstract] In one sentence
> Alpha means every feature exists, and after it no new features are added, only content, fixes and polish.

## What this topic is for

Students resist this line, because the next idea always seems small. The lecture makes the reason concrete: three weeks remain, and every new feature is the least tested part of the game.

## Key ideas

- **Before Alpha** the question is "what else can I add?" **After Alpha** it's "what's left to finish?"
- **Rough is fine; missing is not.** A feature with placeholder art counts. A feature that doesn't exist yet is either finished this week or cut.
- **The must-have list decides.** The Alpha check goes through page 10 of the ten-pager, line by line: there, partly there, or cut.
- **Cutting now is cheap.** Cutting in Week 14 wastes everything spent on the feature since.
- **After the freeze:** levels and content, replacing placeholders, fixing bugs, and polish.

## In class

1. Show the Alpha check for one sample game against its must-have list.
2. Each student marks their own list before the instructor's check.
