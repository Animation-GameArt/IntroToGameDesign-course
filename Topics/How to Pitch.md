# How to Pitch

**Week 2 · [[Lecture] Week 2](https://github.com/Animation-GameArt/IntroToGameDesign-course/blob/main/Class%20Pages/Week%2002%20-%20Lecture.md)**

> [!abstract] In one sentence
> A pitch makes someone else understand and want your game in about two minutes, and in this course it also tells the instructor which kit and custom scripts your game needs.

## What this topic is for

Students write their pitch post during the coming week, and present it live in Week 3. This lecture shows them what a good pitch contains and what makes one vague, so the posts arrive in a form that can be approved quickly.

## Key ideas

- **A pitch answers three questions fast:** What does the player do? Why is it fun? Why this game and not one that already exists?
- **The logline.** One sentence with the player, the action and the hook. "A lighthouse keeper's ghost guides ships through a storm by switching lights on and off, one rock at a time."
- **The core loop.** The actions the player repeats. See [Core Loops](https://github.com/Animation-GameArt/IntroToGameDesign-course/blob/main/Topics/Core%20Loops.md).
- **Comparable games.** "It's like *X* meets *Y*" is quick and useful, as long as the student can say how theirs is different.
- **The mechanics list.** In this course the pitch also lists the game's mechanics. The instructor compares that list with the genre's kit to see what custom scripts the game needs.
- **Specific beats impressive.** "Five levels, three enemy types, one boss" is a stronger pitch than "a huge world full of secrets."

## In class

1. Present one good pitch and one vague one, both for the same idea. Let the class name what the vague one is missing.
2. Walk through the [Pitch](https://github.com/Animation-GameArt/IntroToGameDesign-course/blob/main/Assignments/Pitch.md) assignment's required parts.
3. Have students say their logline to a neighbor, who repeats it back in their own words.

## Common problems

| The pitch… | Ask… |
|---|---|
| Is mostly story | "What does the player do in the first minute?" |
| Lists every feature the student can imagine | "Which three would you keep if you had half the time?" |
| Has no genre from the map | "Which genre is the player doing most of the time?" |
| Names a mechanic the kit doesn't cover | "Is this one of your custom scripts, or can it be cut?" |
