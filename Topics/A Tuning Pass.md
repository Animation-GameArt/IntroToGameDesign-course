# A Tuning Pass

**Week 11 · [[Lecture] Week 11](https://github.com/Animation-GameArt/IntroToGameDesign-course/blob/main/Class%20Pages/Week%2011%20-%20Lecture.md)**

> [!abstract] In one sentence
> A tuning pass changes numbers, not features, until the core loop feels the way the ten-pager describes.

## What this topic is for

The sprint review asks whether the core loop is complete and feels right. Students learn that "feels right" can be tested, and that half an hour of tuning often helps more than a week of new features.

## Key ideas

- **"Feels right" can be checked:** the main action responds at once, the player can tell what happened, and doing it the twentieth time is still satisfying.
- **Pick the main action.** The one the player does most.
- **Change one number at a time while playing,** as in Ex 03: speed, jump height, damage, spawn rate, cost.
- **Write down what works.** Values changed in Play mode are lost; copy the component and paste its values after stopping.
- **Tune against the ten-pager.** It says what the game should feel like: tense, cozy, frantic. Tune toward that word.
- **If two minutes of the loop is boring,** more content won't fix it.

## In class

1. Each student names the one thing in their sprint review that doesn't feel right.
2. Thirty minutes of studio time go to tuning only that.
