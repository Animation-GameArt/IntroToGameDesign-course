# Running Pitch Day

**Week 3 · [[Lecture] Week 3](https://github.com/Animation-GameArt/IntroToGameDesign-course/blob/main/Class%20Pages/Week%2003%20-%20Lecture.md) · Instructor notes**

> [!abstract] In one sentence
> Every student presents their posted pitch in about two minutes and leaves knowing whether it's approved, approved with conditions, or needs a revision.

## What this is for

Pitch Day turns ideas into approved projects. It also gives the instructor what's needed to prepare kits, custom scripts and per-game tutorials during Weeks 3 and 4.

## How it runs

1. **Before class.** Pitch posts are due in the Canvas Discussion by 11:59 PM the night before. Read them before class and note, for each one, the genre, the kit, and any mechanic the kit doesn't cover.
2. **Presentations.** About two minutes each, presented from the post. No slides.
3. **Feedback.** Two questions only: is the core loop clear, and does the scope fit eight weeks in a supported genre?
4. **A decision, said out loud:**

| Decision | Means |
|---|---|
| Approved | Go on to the one-sheet |
| Approved with conditions | Named changes, such as a cut or a genre change, must appear in the one-sheet |
| Revise | Rework and repost by a set date |

5. **Custom scripts.** Agree the list of mechanics the kit doesn't cover, within the course's limit per student. These become the student's custom scripts.

## After class

- Record each decision and condition.
- Group the approved pitches by kit. That decides which kits must be ready first.
- Prepare each game's custom scripts and short guide during Weeks 3 and 4.
